# 07 Launch QA Checklist

Before production launch:
- Run lint, typecheck, content check, and build.
- Verify homepage, shop, product detail, cart, checkout, and COA pages.
- Confirm top banner, product card, and checkout attestation placements.
- Confirm Vial catalog endpoint paths in staging before production.
- Confirm Vial order payload and idempotency behavior before accepting orders.
- Confirm payment approval happens before Vial fulfillment submission.
- Confirm policy pages have legal/compliance review.
