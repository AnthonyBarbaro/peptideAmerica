# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, lab purchasers, and operational buyers who need to browse a research catalog, review product identifiers, check COA availability, manage carts, and track orders without friction.

## Product Purpose

Peptide America is a Vial-backed research catalog and commerce frontend. It should make catalog browsing, documentation lookup, checkout handoff, account access, and order tracking feel reliable, direct, and production-ready.

## Brand Personality

Clean, precise, restrained. The interface should feel premium and trustworthy without adding hype, medical framing, or decorative science clutter.

## Anti-references

Avoid mock-storefront language, WooCommerce patterns, developer notes in customer-facing UI, oversized dashboards, dense technical tables for simple shopping tasks, over-stylized AI design patterns, excessive pills, unnecessary cards, and medical, dosing, human-use, animal-use, outcome, or performance claims.

## Design Principles

1. Keep catalog tasks obvious: search, filter, compare, add to cart, and review COA should be easy to find.
2. Prefer standard ecommerce controls over invented interactions.
3. Use the navy, white, and red brand palette with restraint.
4. Let Vial, Clerk, Directus, and payment boundaries stay invisible unless the user needs a status.
5. Preserve compliance by keeping product copy factual and research-only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target keyboard-usable, responsive, high-contrast interfaces with visible focus states, readable labels, reduced-motion support, and no information conveyed by color alone.
